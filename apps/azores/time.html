Conversions use the browser's own time zone database, so daylight saving is handled on both ends. From US Pacific the gap is 7 hours almost all year, because both places shift together — but the Azores leave summer time about a week before the US does, and in that gap it is 6 hours, not 7. Pick a date in late October and the grid shows it. “Typical workday” here means 8 AM through the 5 PM hour, local, at both ends.
