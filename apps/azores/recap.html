Aug 24 – Sep 1, 2026 · São Miguel

How it actually went.

The plan was three places to sleep and a mostly empty calendar, decided each morning off the forecast. That held up — but almost nothing landed on the day it was pencilled in for, the rain was heavier and more frequent than expected, and the island turned out to need far more booking ahead than a plan built on spontaneity assumed.

Best thing we did

Whale watching, without much competition. Dolphins with pink bellies jumping and feeding, chasing birds alongside the boat, then four sperm whales — the last two raising their tails as they went down. The Zodiac took rain and big waves to get there and it was still the clear favourite of the trip.

Day by day

What we liked

Nature and water

  • Whale watching — dolphins and four sperm whales, two tail sightings. The best thing we did.
  • Lagoa do Fogo, twice: windy and clear by bus, then again on a genuinely sunny day.
  • Black-sand beaches, waterfalls, ocean overlooks, and the hike down to the lake.
  • Whaler's Cove — the historic place the whales were brought in, one steep section on the way down.

Food

  • Wood-fired and Azores-style steak, with garlic and salted red pepper. Ordered medium rare, arrived considerably rarer.
  • White cod — oily and much better than that sounds. Fried cod and the tuna too.
  • Cheese boards. The three-cheese platter with spicy pepper jam, and cheese generally as a regional thing.
  • The late 9:30pm dinner on the last full night — pizza, steak, crème brûlée, and Neapolitan ice cream where the strawberry stood out.

Hotels and kit

  • All three had extensive breakfasts. The third had the best breakfast and the best views; Furnas was the overall favourite — comfortable, and "White Lotus vibes".
  • The packing list held: linen and cotton, a rain jacket, swimsuits, hair ties, battery banks, two pairs of shoes.
  • Ray-Ban Meta glasses for photos and a Garmin for tracking both earned their place.

What surprised us

You have to book ahead

  • Restaurants, wineries and excursions wanted reservations — including restaurants with visibly empty tables.
  • Bookings often ran over WhatsApp rather than a website or a phone call.
  • The plan was deliberately built to stay loose. On this island that was the wrong instinct.

Weather and roads

  • Cool rain to humid heat within minutes, repeatedly. Rain was frequent and heavy, not the short bursts we'd read about.
  • Cobblestones, no sidewalks, fast traffic, close tailgating. The car was necessary and stressful in equal measure.

Small things

  • Pineapples take about two and a half years. Two greenhouse visits, and only 30–40 plants visible at a time — the real farming must happen somewhere else.
  • Level 5 of the treetop course was far harder than the rest, and Level 6 was easy straight after it.
  • Everyday differences: breakfast, minimal-makeup norms, espresso after meals, and cheese, fish, stewed meats, rice and potatoes everywhere.

What we would change

Spontaneity did not work

  • The one thing the plan was built around turned out to be the thing the island least rewarded.
  • The winery never happened — wineries are closed at weekends.
  • Pete's Volcanic Coffee could not be found at all: the website and the Instagram pin gave the same wrong location, and we crossed the same river repeatedly looking. It may have burned down.

Driving and getting about

  • A further 1.5-hour wait for the rental car, after arriving early, dropping bags, and coming back to the airport for it.
  • Road conditions, tailgating, narrow streets, and hectic traffic on the scooters — which topped out at 20 km/h.
  • The rental car clicked the entire trip.

Misses and mishaps

  • Two beaches in a row turned out to be concrete slabs, not sand — the second after walking about four miles to reach it.
  • The buffet lunch of stewed pork and clams probably explains the stomach trouble that followed.
  • The whale figurine was €53, against an expected €10–15. It was suggested it might be actual whale bone.
  • Music near the Furnas hotel ran to 2am and made sleeping hard.
