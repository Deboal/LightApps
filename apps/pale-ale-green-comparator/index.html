How the comparison works

Colors are converted from sRGB to CIE L*a*b*. Ranking uses ΔE 1976, a basic perceptual distance where smaller is closer. “Auto-detect” selects reasonably saturated green pixels (green hue, excluding very dark/bright pixels) and takes a robust median. For glossy cans, shadows, highlights, and camera white balance can change apparent color, so use several photos and click representative printed green areas when possible.
